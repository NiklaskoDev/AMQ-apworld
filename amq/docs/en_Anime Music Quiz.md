# Anime Music Quiz

## What is this?

[Anime Music Quiz](https://animemusicquiz.com) is a free browser game in which you guess the anime behind short
opening, ending and insert song clips. This world turns the *lobby filters* into progression: song types, decades,
genres, anime formats and difficulty tiers are locked until you find the matching item. Your own correct guesses
send checks.

This is an unaffiliated fan project. It contains no anime titles, song names, artist names, audio, video or artwork.
It only uses generic categories ("Opening", "1990s", "Comedy", ...).

## What is randomized?

Items are unlocks that live in the multiworld:

* **Song passes**: Opening / Ending / Insert Song
* **Decade passes**: 1980s & Earlier, 1990s, 2000s, 2010s, 2020s
* **Genre keys**: 14 broad genres
* **Format passes**: Movie, OVA, ONA, Special (TV is always unlocked)
* **Progressive Rarity Lens** (x3): unlocks Medium, then Hard, then Brutal songs (by community guess rate)
* **Mixtape Fragments**: collect enough to open the Grand Finale and, for the later stages, to get into new stages

## What is a check?

Everything is detected from songs you answer in the real game:

* Cumulative correct answers (10, 20, 30, ...)
* Correct songs per type, decade, genre, format and difficulty tier
* Hot streaks and flawless games
* Single-game variety challenges: Triple Threat (all three song types), Time Capsule (several decades),
  Genre Sampler (several genres)
* Marathon games and high-accuracy games
* Optional multiplayer placements

Songs that fall outside your current unlocks are ignored: they don't count for checks and don't break streaks.

## Other items

* **Encore Ticket**: your next correct answer counts twice toward cumulative milestones
* **Streak Shield**: absorbs your next wrong answer for streak purposes
* **Hype Cheer**: pure applause

## Traps

Applied by the userscript for a number of songs (configurable):

* **Mute Trap**: the audio is muted
* **Reverse Typing Trap**: the answer box displays text backwards
* **Tiny Text Trap**: the answer box shrinks to near-unreadable size
* **Streak Wipe Trap**: your current streak resets
* **Tax Collector Trap**: you lose 5 points of cumulative milestone progress

## Goal

Collect the required Mixtape Fragments. With the `finale` goal you must then also win one finale game
(a minimum number of eligible songs at a minimum accuracy).
